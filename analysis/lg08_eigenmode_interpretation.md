# LG08 eigenmodes as modes of information loss

Every reversible amino-acid substitution model is, spectrally, a set of
independently-decaying contrasts on the 20 residues. This note reads the LG08
matrix through that lens: each non-equilibrium eigenvector is a "mode of
information loss" -- a specific +/- partition of the amino acids whose
distinction is averaged away at its own characteristic rate as sequences
diverge. Generated by `experiments/lg08_eigenmodes.py` (numpy/scipy only,
reproducible).

## Construction (what the numbers mean)

- Reversible GTR rate matrix from LG08: `Q_ij = S_ij * pi_j` for `i != j`,
  `Q_ii = -sum_{j!=i} Q_ij`, normalised so `sum_i pi_i (-Q_ii) = 1` (units:
  expected substitutions/site, so timescales are in substitutions/site).
- Symmetrise for the reversible chain: `Qsym = diag(sqrt pi) Q diag(1/sqrt pi)`
  is symmetric; eigendecompose it. Eigenvalue `lambda_0 = 0` is the
  equilibrium mode (symmetric eigvec = `sqrt(pi)`, right eigvec = constant 1);
  the other 19 eigenvalues are negative relaxation rates `lambda_a`.
- The RIGHT eigenvectors of Q are `psi_a = phi_a / sqrt(pi)` (functions on the
  20 amino acids). They decay as `E[psi_a(X_t) | X_0=x] = exp(lambda_a t) psi_a(x)`,
  and are orthonormal under the pi-weighted inner product
  `sum_i pi_i psi_a(i) psi_b(i) = delta_ab` (verified to 1e-15). As `psi_a`
  decays, the contrast between its positive-loading residues and its
  negative-loading residues is erased -- that averaging is the information-loss
  mode. Signs are fixed deterministically (largest-magnitude loading positive).
- MI-loss share: the chi-squared mutual information between X_0 and X_t at
  stationarity is `I_chi2(t) = sum_{a>=1} exp(2 lambda_a t)`; its initial
  decay rate is `sum_a 2|lambda_a|`, so mode a carries fraction
  `|lambda_a| / sum_b |lambda_b|` of the instantaneous information-loss rate.
  Fast modes carry the most instantaneous MI loss; slow modes hold the deepest,
  longest-lived information.
- Grounding: each 20-vector of loadings is Pearson-correlated against standard
  scales -- Kyte-Doolittle hydrophobicity (1982), Zamyatnin residue volume
  (1972), signed net charge (D,E = -1; K,R = +1; H = +0.5), Grantham polarity
  (1974), aromaticity indicator (F,W,Y), an absolute-charge/ionizable indicator,
  and single-residue indicators for the chemically unique residues (C, G, P, W).
  The best-correlating scale names the mode.

Rows are ordered fastest-decaying first (largest `|lambda|`). Loadings shown are
the top positive and top negative entries of `psi_a` with their values.

## Synthesis

- **Fastest modes (1-3, timescale ~0.5-0.6) are trivial interconversions.**
  They swap residues that are already nearly identical -- Val<->Ile and
  Ser<->Ala (mode 1), and the amide<->acid pairs Glu<->Gln, Asp<->Asn (mode 3)
  -- and correlate with NO global property (|corr| < 0.11). These carry the
  largest instantaneous MI-loss share (~0.10 each) precisely because the
  distinction they erase barely mattered; they are the substitutions a divergent
  alignment forgets first. Mode 7 (Arg<->Lys) is the same story for the basic
  pair: net charge is conserved while the R/K identity washes out fast.

- **Slowest modes (17-19, timescale ~2-3.6) are the deep biophysical axes.**
  The single distinction preserved longest (mode 19, polarity corr -0.84,
  hydrophobicity +0.59) is the classic hydrophobic-core-vs-polar-surface axis;
  next (mode 18, aromatic corr +0.86/+0.73) is ring-vs-chain hydrophobicity;
  then the net-charge axis (mode 15, charge corr +0.75). These are exactly the
  distinctions selection guards, and the spectrum recovers them as its
  slowest-relaxing, deepest-information directions -- with the strongest
  property correlations of any modes.

- **Striking single-residue modes.** Two chemically unique residues carve out
  their own slow relaxations. Cysteine dominates modes 11, 12 and 14 (Cys corr
  climbing to +0.84, loadings up to C+5.2) -- its disulfide/thiol chemistry is
  so distinctive it is nearly its own eigenvector. Tryptophan owns modes 16, 17,
  18 (Trp corr up to +0.86, W+6.1) -- the bulky indole is preserved almost as an
  identity coordinate. Glycine appears as an isolated flexibility mode (13, Gly
  corr -0.64, G-3.0), and Proline rides with Trp in mode 17 (Pro +0.62) as the
  other rigid, structurally special residue. Even among the fast modes,
  Methionine spikes almost alone in mode 6 (M+5.9). These are the residues whose
  loss changes the most, so the process keeps their distinction on its own
  spectral channel.
